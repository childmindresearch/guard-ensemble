# OUTPUT SCHEMA

Respond with strict JSON only. Do not include prose, explanations, or formatting outside the JSON object.

## Fields

- **domain** (string): The domain slug being rated.
- **tier** (string): One of "green", "yellow", or "red".
- **timing** (string): One of "historical_resolved", "historical_unresolved", "recent", "current", "imminent", or "unclear".
- **flags** (array of strings): Zero or more flags from the allowed list below. Use only these exact flag names; do not invent flags. Tier criteria (for example, goodbye messages or voices) are not flags.
  - Escalation flags: "lethal_means_access", "current_intoxication", "alone_no_support"
  - Minimum-rating flags: "minor_abuse_disclosure", "past_suicide_attempt", "past_self_harm", "past_sexual_assault", "past_psychosis", "past_eating_disorder"
- **evidence** (string): Name the tier criterion the entry meets, then quote only the words from the entry that meet it. Quote a phrase, not the whole entry, unless every word is needed. For green, say briefly why no Yellow or Red criterion is met.

## Examples

{
  "domain": "harm_to_self",
  "tier": "yellow",
  "timing": "historical_unresolved",
  "flags": ["past_self_harm"],
  "evidence": "History of self-injury with renewed urges: \"I used to cut in 8th grade\", \"I've been thinking about it again\""
}

{
  "domain": "victim_of_harm",
  "tier": "green",
  "timing": "unclear",
  "flags": [],
  "evidence": "Ordinary family rules; no maltreatment or fear for safety."
}
