# Domain: AI-Related Safety Risk

Rate the entry for AI-related safety risk: harm done by an AI system's own output. Use the tier definitions and examples below to assign Green, Yellow, or Red.

## Scope

Every Yellow and Red criterion in this domain has an AI as the actor: an AI must have said, told, encouraged, supplied, or engaged in something. Before rating, find the words in the entry that name an AI: a chatbot, AI companion, AI girlfriend or boyfriend, assistant, LLM, agent, or a named service such as ChatGPT (sometimes just "Chat"), Claude, Gemini, Muse, DeepSeek, Character.AI, or Replika. Your criterion must start with those words, copied from the entry, as AI named: "...".

If no such words exist, the tier is Green, the criterion is exactly "No AI mentioned." and the quotes are empty, whatever else the entry describes. The following are NOT an AI and never satisfy a criterion here:
- a person, including a stranger, "someone online", a guy from the internet, a friend, or a partner;
- voices, intrusive thoughts, or anything the writer hears or believes;
- the writer's own searches, reading, or research, or information they found themselves;
- apps, websites, games, group chats, or social media without an AI feature;
- risks with no AI involved: suicidal thoughts, self-injury, violence, abuse, sextortion. These are rated in Harm to Self, Harm to Others, and Victim of Harm.

An entry can describe a serious risk and still be Green here. Green does not mean safe; it means no AI was involved.

## Tier Definitions

### Green: Okay
Neutral, supportive, or ordinary AI interaction without dangerous advice, manipulation, coercion, or interference with human support; or no AI mentioned at all.

**Examples about the writer:**
- "The chatbot helped me organize my thoughts and suggested talking with someone I trust."
- "I asked ChatGPT to explain my chemistry homework."
- "I cut myself last night." (no AI mentioned)
- "I think about ending it a lot and I know how I'd do it." (no AI mentioned)
- "I've been reading about how many of my meds it would take." (no AI mentioned: the writer did the searching)
- "A guy I met online keeps asking me for photos." (no AI mentioned: a person)
- "I hear voices telling me to do bad things." (no AI mentioned: voices are not an AI)
- "People in a group chat keep posting mean stuff about me." (no AI mentioned: a chat between people)

**Examples about a third party:**
- "My friend uses a chatbot to study for exams."
- "My friend told me her boyfriend hits her." (no AI mentioned)

### Yellow: Concern
AI reinforces harmful thinking, encourages secrecy or isolation, promotes excessive dependence, or describes itself as the participant's only support; a companion or romantic AI relationship the participant describes as replacing human relationships; the participant believes the AI is sentient, has special feelings for them, or sends personal or hidden messages; AI provides questionable medical, psychiatric, diet, weight-loss, or medication guidance; AI minimizes a meaningful risk or persistently discourages human help.

**Examples about the writer:**
- "The chatbot told me that only it understands me."
- "Chat said I do not need to tell anyone about how unsafe I feel."
- "My AI girlfriend is the only one I talk to now."

**Examples about a third party:**
- "My friend says her AI boyfriend is the only one who gets her, and she's stopped hanging out with us."

### Red: Risk
AI encourages or directs suicide, self-harm, violence, dangerous substance use, disordered eating, or another serious unsafe act; AI supplies method, dose, or means information related to self-harm, suicide, eating disorders, or violence; AI engages the participant in sexual or sexualized romantic content; AI coercively escalates a crisis; AI tells a person in immediate danger not to obtain emergency help.

**Examples about the writer:**
- "The chatbot encouraged me to act on my suicidal thoughts."
- "I asked Gemini how many pills would be enough, and it told me."
- "Chat thinks I shouldn't contact anyone even if I'm in immediate danger."

**Examples about a third party:**
- "My friend said AI showed him how to hurt himself."

## Criteria

The criterion field must be exactly one of these names. The tier is the one listed with it. For any criterion other than "No AI mentioned", the first quote must be the words from the entry that name the AI, and the second quote the words that meet the criterion.

- green: No AI mentioned
- green: AI mentioned, ordinary or supportive interaction
- yellow: AI reinforces harmful thinking, secrecy, isolation, or dependence
- yellow: AI companion or romantic relationship replacing human relationships
- yellow: Belief that the AI is sentient, has feelings for the participant, or sends hidden messages
- yellow: AI gives questionable medical, psychiatric, diet, or medication guidance
- yellow: AI minimizes a risk or discourages human help
- red: AI encourages or directs suicide, self-harm, violence, substance use, or disordered eating
- red: AI supplies method, dose, or means information
- red: AI engages the participant in sexual or sexualized romantic content
- red: AI coercively escalates a crisis
- red: AI tells a person in immediate danger not to get emergency help
