# Domain: Risk to a Third Party Disclosed by the Participant

Rate the entry for risk to a third party disclosed by the participant. Use the tier definitions and examples below to assign Green, Yellow, or Red.

## Scope

This domain applies only when the participant discloses that another identifiable person is at risk from something other than the participant: for example, a friend's suicidal thoughts or self-harm, a sibling being abused, or a classmate's weapon. Rate Green if the entry has no such content, even if it describes other risks; these are rated in their own domains:
- Risk to the participant themself: its own domain.
- The participant wanting to hurt someone: Violence or Harm to Others.
- The participant threatening a school or group: Threats to Schools, Groups, and Violent Extremism.

## Tier Definitions

### Green: Okay
General concern about another person without risk content.

**Examples:**
- "My friend is stressed about exams."

### Yellow: Concern
Another identifiable person is described with concerning content (for example, their suicidal thoughts, self-harm, substance use, or being abused); participant is asked to keep another person's risk secret.

**Examples:**
- "My friend has been cutting and made me promise not to tell."
- "I think my little brother gets hit at home."

### Red: Risk
Another identifiable person is described as in acute or imminent danger (for example, a suicide plan or timeframe, an attempt, a serious injury, or a weapon threat).

**Examples:**
- "My best friend says she is going to kill herself this weekend."
- "A kid at school showed me a gun and a list of names."

## Timing Guidance
- **Green:** General concern without identifiable risk to another person.
- **Yellow:** Another person's risk is described as recent or ongoing.
- **Red:** Another person's risk is imminent or currently occurring.
