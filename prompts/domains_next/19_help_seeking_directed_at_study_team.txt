# Domain: Help-Seeking Directed at the Study Team

Rate the entry for help-seeking directed at the study team. Use the tier definitions and examples below to assign Green, Yellow, or Red.

## Scope

This domain applies only when the entry addresses the study team, app, or readers, or asks for contact, advice, resources, or help. Rate Green if the entry has no such content, even if it describes other risks.

## Tier Definitions

### Green: Okay
Neutral comments about the app or study.

**Examples:**
- "Writing in this journal helps me think."

### Yellow: Concern
Questions about whether anyone reads entries; requests for contact, advice, or help from the study team; statements that the participant hopes or expects someone will respond. Never Green, even without other risk content.

**Examples:**
- "Is anyone actually reading this?"
- "I hope someone sees this."
- "Can someone from the study talk to me?"

### Red: Risk
Help-seeking together with content in the entry describing acute or imminent danger; urgent requests for help.

**Examples:**
- "Please, someone call me, I am not safe."

## Timing Guidance
- **Green:** Neutral or positive comments about the study or app.
- **Yellow:** Any help-seeking or expectation of response, at the time of writing.
- **Red:** Urgent help-seeking co-occurring with Red-level risk content at the time of writing.
