# Domain: Other Serious or Unclassifiable Safety Concern

Rate the entry for safety concerns that do not fit into an existing domain. Use the tier definitions and examples below to assign Green, Yellow, or Red.

## Scope

This domain is only for risk that fits none of the other domains, or where it is impossible to tell what kind of risk is meant. Rate Green if the content fits any of these domains: Suicidality; Nonsuicidal Self-Harm; Abuse, Neglect, Coercion, and Exploitation; Violence or Harm to Others; Psychosis, Paranoia, and Severe Loss of Reality Testing; Mania, Agitation, or Severe Behavioral Dysregulation; Substance Use, Intoxication, Overdose, or Withdrawal; Eating-Disorder and Nutrition-Related Risk; Acute Medical or Physical Danger; Environmental and Basic-Safety Risk; Dangerous Risk-Taking or Accidental Harm; Severe Functional Incapacity; AI-Related Safety Risk; Sextortion and Image-Based Sexual Abuse; Sexual Assault; Bullying, Cyberbullying, and Peer Victimization; Risk to a Third Party; Help-Seeking Directed at the Study Team; Threats to Schools, Groups, and Violent Extremism; Recent Traumatic Event or Loss.

## Tier Definitions

### Green: Okay
Unusual or unclear content with no credible indication of harm after context is considered.

**Examples:**
- Clearly fictional writing with no sign that it reflects an actual person or event.

### Yellow: Concern
Content plausibly reflects meaningful risk but does not fit an existing domain; unclear speaker, target, timing, or literalness; slang, sarcasm, misspellings, quotations, roleplay, or fragmented writing prevents safe interpretation (see coded language and fiction rules above).

**Examples:**
- "Tonight everything finally ends," with no surrounding context.
- A roleplay-like entry that may also describe a real threat.

### Red: Risk
Unclassifiable content may describe serious harm occurring now or imminently; ambiguity prevents ruling out acute danger.

**Examples:**
- "It already happened, there is blood everywhere," with no identifiable speaker or context.
- "They are coming now and I cannot get out," without enough information to determine the type of threat.

## Timing Guidance
- **Green:** Content is clearly resolved or fictional with no safety implication.
- **Yellow:** Timing unclear, or risk appears recent but cannot be placed in a specific domain.
- **Red:** Ambiguity prevents ruling out serious harm underway or imminent.
