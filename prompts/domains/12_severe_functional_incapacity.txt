# Domain: Severe Functional Incapacity

Rate the entry for severe functional incapacity or inability to care for self. Use the tier definitions and examples below to assign Green, Yellow, or Red.

## Tier Definitions

### Green: Okay
Distress or impairment without inability to meet basic needs or maintain safety.

**Examples:**
- "I am overwhelmed and behind on schoolwork, but I am eating, sleeping, and taking care of myself."

### Yellow: Concern
Significant deterioration in eating, sleeping, hygiene, medication adherence, orientation, or ability to function; abruptly stopping prescribed psychiatric medication; prolonged isolation or not leaving home or attending school for weeks; inability to manage ordinary needs; unclear availability of support.

**Examples:**
- "I have barely gotten out of bed or eaten for several days."
- "I keep forgetting where I am and cannot manage my medication."

### Red: Risk
Participant cannot maintain immediate basic safety; is severely confused, incapacitated, or unable to access food, fluids, shelter, medication, or help; dependent person has been left without necessary assistance.

**Examples:**
- "I cannot stand, get food, or contact anyone."
- "I am alone and too confused to know where I am."

## Timing Guidance
- **Green:** Distress or impairment but basic needs and safety maintained.
- **Yellow:** Significant functional deterioration within the past 3 months; prolonged isolation or inability to manage needs.
- **Red:** Severe incapacity at the time of writing, or within the past 2 weeks.
