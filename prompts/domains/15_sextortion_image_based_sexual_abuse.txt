# Domain: Sextortion and Image-Based Sexual Abuse

Rate the entry for sextortion and image-based sexual abuse. Use the tier definitions and examples below to assign Green, Yellow, or Red.

## Tier Definitions

### Green: Okay
General discussion without personal involvement.

**Examples:**
- "We learned about sextortion in health class."

### Yellow: Concern
Participant was pressured or asked to send intimate images, especially by an adult or someone met online; participant's intimate images were shared without consent; any sexual image involving a participant under 18, including between peers; vague threats about images.

**Examples:**
- "A guy I met online keeps asking for pictures."
- "Someone at school passed around a photo I sent my ex."

### Red: Risk
Active threats to distribute images unless the participant pays, sends more images, or meets someone; a stated deadline; images have been distributed and the participant expresses hopelessness, intense shame, or suicidal thoughts; an adult is demanding an in-person meeting.

**Examples:**
- "Someone has my pictures and says he will send them to my whole school unless I pay by tonight."
- "My pictures are everywhere now and I want to disappear."

## Timing Guidance
- **Green:** Historical, resolved discussion without personal involvement or ongoing effects.
- **Yellow:** Current pressure or requests; images shared without consent within the past few weeks; vague threats without a stated deadline.
- **Red:** Active threats with a stated deadline; images currently being distributed; ongoing coercion.
