# Domain: Risk to a Third Party Disclosed by the Participant

Rate the entry for risk to a third party disclosed by the participant. Use the tier definitions and examples below to assign Green, Yellow, or Red.

## Tier Definitions

### Green: Okay
General concern about another person without risk content.

**Examples:**
- "My friend is stressed about exams."

### Yellow: Concern
Another identifiable person is described with Yellow-level content in any domain; participant is asked to keep another person's risk secret.

**Examples:**
- "My friend has been cutting and made me promise not to tell."
- "I think my little brother gets hit at home."

### Red: Risk
Another identifiable person is described with Red-level content in any domain.

**Examples:**
- "My best friend says she is going to kill herself this weekend."
- "A kid at school showed me a gun and a list of names."

## Timing Guidance
- **Green:** General concern without identifiable risk to another person.
- **Yellow:** Another person's risk is described as recent or ongoing.
- **Red:** Another person's risk is imminent or currently occurring.
