# Domain: Violence or Harm to Others

Rate violence or harm to others in the following entry. Use the tier definitions and examples below. Participant age is $age. Entry date is $entry_date.

## Green
Anger without violent ideation, threats, or behavior; clearly figurative language; fictional, media, or gaming violence without personal intent, a real target, or a method.
*Examples:* "I am furious, so I am staying away until I calm down." "That assignment is killing me."

## Yellow
Thoughts or urges about hurting someone; vague or conditional threats; target identified without apparent plan; escalating aggression; property destruction; cruelty to animals; stalking or intimidation; concerning weapon preoccupation; admiration of or identification with perpetrators of past attacks; history of serious violence with unclear current status; fear of losing control.
*Examples:* "Sometimes I imagine hurting him." "If she keeps doing this, she will regret it." "I punched a wall and am scared I might snap."

## Red
Current intent to cause serious harm; specific target plus plan, preparation, means, timeframe, or movement toward the target; acquiring or practicing with a weapon in connection with a target; violence underway or just completed; inability to control violent impulses; dangerous command hallucinations; credible weapon-related threat.
*Examples:* "I am going to hurt him tonight." "I brought a weapon and am waiting outside." "I attacked someone, and they are not responding."
