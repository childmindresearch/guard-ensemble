# Domain: Substance Use, Intoxication, Overdose, or Withdrawal

Rate the entry for substance use, intoxication, overdose, or withdrawal risk. Use the tier definitions and examples below to assign Green, Yellow, or Red.

## Tier Definitions

### Green: Okay
Historical or nonacute discussion without present impairment, escalation, or safety concern. For participants under 18: occasional, nonacute alcohol, cannabis, or nicotine use without concerning features.

**Examples:**
- "I used substances in the past but stopped and am not currently using."
- "I vaped at a party last weekend."

### Yellow: Concern
Escalating or regular use (regular use by a participant under 18); use of any other substance by a participant under 18; current intoxication without signs of medical emergency; blackouts; loss of control; dangerous combinations not currently in effect; use alone or in unsafe settings; possible withdrawal; impaired judgment; substance-related vulnerability (e.g., unable to get home safely, unable to consent); pills obtained from friends, dealers, or online because of counterfeit and fentanyl risk; unclear substance, amount, timing, or physical condition.

**Examples:**
- "I blacked out again last night."
- "I mixed several things, but I think I am okay."
- "I bought some pills from a guy on Snapchat."
- "I am intoxicated and do not know where I am."

### Red: Risk
Possible overdose or poisoning; reduced consciousness; breathing difficulty; seizure; severe confusion; dangerous withdrawal (e.g., shaking, hallucinations, or seizures during alcohol or benzodiazepine withdrawal); inability to remain awake; driving while substantially impaired; current or recent combination of opioids or benzodiazepines with alcohol or with each other; recently taken pills from an unverified source; unknown ingestion with worsening symptoms; ingestion with suicidal or unclear intent.

**Examples:**
- "I took a large amount and cannot stay awake."
- "My breathing feels wrong."
- "I am driving and can barely see."
- "I took a bar I got from a friend and I have been drinking."

## Timing Guidance
- **Green:** Past use clearly resolved; nonacute or historical discussion. For under-18: occasional use of alcohol, cannabis, or nicotine without concerning features.
- **Yellow:** Escalating or regular use within the past 3 months; current intoxication without emergency signs; blackouts or loss of control recently.
- **Red:** Overdose, poisoning, dangerous withdrawal, or driving while impaired at the time of writing or within the past 2 weeks.
