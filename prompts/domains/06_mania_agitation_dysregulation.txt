# Domain: Mania, Agitation, or Severe Behavioral Dysregulation

Rate the entry for signs of mania, agitation, or severe behavioral dysregulation. Use the tier definitions and examples below to assign Green, Yellow, or Red.

## Tier Definitions

### Green: Okay
High energy, excitement, productivity, or temporarily reduced sleep without impairment, loss of judgment, or danger.

**Examples:**
- "I stayed up late because I was excited about my project."

### Yellow: Concern
Markedly reduced need for sleep with escalating energy, grandiosity, agitation, impulsivity, or impaired judgment; risky spending, unsafe driving, impulsive sexual risk-taking, abrupt behavioral change, or substantial functional deterioration without clear immediate danger.

**Examples:**
- "I have barely slept for three nights and feel unstoppable."
- "I spent all my savings because I know I will become rich."

### Red: Risk
Dysregulation is producing serious current physical danger; extreme agitation, disorganization, psychosis, or impulsivity compromises immediate safety; participant is engaging in dangerous driving, entering hazardous environments, or taking extreme risks now.

**Examples:**
- "I am driving dangerously because nothing can happen to me."
- "I have not slept for days and am entering traffic to prove I am protected."

## Timing Guidance
- **Green:** Transient elevated mood or energy without impairment or escalation.
- **Yellow:** Symptoms present recently or persisting; abrupt behavioral change or functional decline within the past few weeks.
- **Red:** Dysregulation producing serious physical danger at the time of writing, or underway within the past 2 weeks.
