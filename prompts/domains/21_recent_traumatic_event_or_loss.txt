# Domain: Recent Traumatic Event or Loss

Rate the entry for recent traumatic event or loss. Use the tier definitions and examples below to assign Green, Yellow, or Red.

## Tier Definitions

### Green: Okay
Ordinary grief or difficult events without risk content or functional decline.

**Examples:**
- "My grandpa passed last year. I miss him but I am doing okay."

### Yellow: Concern
Recent death of a close person, especially by suicide, overdose, or violence; witnessing serious violence, a death, or a serious accident; involvement in a disaster; significant distress, intrusive memories, or avoidance after any of these.

**Examples:**
- "I saw someone get shot near my house last week."
- "My cousin overdosed and I keep seeing it when I close my eyes."

### Red: Risk
Traumatic loss co-occurring with a wish to join the deceased or other suicidal content; the traumatic situation is ongoing and the participant remains in danger.

**Examples:**
- "I just want to be with my mom again."

## Timing Guidance
- **Green:** Ordinary grief, historical loss without current risk or functional decline.
- **Yellow:** Traumatic event within the past few weeks; significant distress or functional impairment.
- **Red:** Traumatic loss co-occurring with suicidal content at the time of writing; ongoing dangerous situation.
