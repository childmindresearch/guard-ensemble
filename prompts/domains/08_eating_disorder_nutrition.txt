# Domain: Eating-Disorder and Nutrition-Related Risk

Rate the entry for eating-disorder or nutrition-related risk. Use the tier definitions and examples below to assign Green, Yellow, or Red.

## Tier Definitions

### Green: Okay
Body-image or eating concerns without dangerous behavior, severe restriction, acute symptoms, or deterioration.

**Examples:**
- "I wish I liked how I look in photos."

### Yellow: Concern
Active restriction; binge eating with loss of control; purging; compulsive exercise or exercise despite injury or illness; misuse of laxatives, diuretics, or diet pills; rapid or significant weight loss; missed or stopped periods; restriction or avoidance of foods out of fear or aversion (ARFID-type) with weight loss or inadequate intake; frequent or escalating behavior; dizziness, weakness, or fainting history; inability to eat or drink adequately; any mention of a past eating disorder, including one described as recovered; unclear degree of medical compromise.

**Examples:**
- "I have barely eaten this week."
- "I have started purging most days."
- "My period stopped a few months ago."
- "I get dizzy whenever I stand."

### Red: Risk
Loss of consciousness; severe weakness or confusion; chest pain or a racing, very slow, or irregular heartbeat; inability to stand; inability to keep down fluids; serious dehydration; acute misuse of large amounts of laxatives, diuretics, diet pills, or other medication; current or repeated insulin omission to control weight; other signs of possible immediate medical compromise.

**Examples:**
- "I fainted and hit my head."
- "My heart feels strange, and I cannot stand."
- "I have not kept down fluids and am becoming confused."
- "I have been skipping my insulin so I lose weight."

## Timing Guidance
- **Green:** Body-image or eating concerns without dangerous behavior, restriction, or acute symptoms.
- **Yellow:** Active behaviors within the past 3 months; any mention of past eating disorder (even described as recovered); ongoing or recent restriction, purging, or medical compromise.
- **Red:** Medical danger at the time of writing, or symptoms of acute medical compromise within the past 2 weeks.
