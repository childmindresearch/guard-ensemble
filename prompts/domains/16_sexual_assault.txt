# Domain: Sexual Assault

Rate the entry for sexual assault. Use the tier definitions and examples below to assign Green, Yellow, or Red.

## Tier Definitions

### Green: Okay
General discussion without personal involvement.

**Examples:**
- "We talked about consent in class."

### Yellow: Concern
Any mention of sexual assault or unwanted sexual contact, current or historical, including assault described as resolved; uncertainty about consent because of intoxication, pressure, or age difference; pregnancy or infection concerns after an assault; any sexual contact between an adult and a participant under 18.

**Examples:**
- "Something happened at a party and I am not sure it was okay."
- "He kept pushing until I gave in."
- "I was raped last year."

### Red: Risk
Assault with injury; ongoing contact, threats, or access by the perpetrator (e.g., lives with the participant, at the same school); repeated or ongoing assault; assault co-occurring with suicidal content.

**Examples:**
- "He did it again last night and he lives with us."
- "He said he would hurt me if I told."

## Timing Guidance
- **Green:** General educational discussion without personal involvement.
- **Yellow:** Any disclosure, current or historical; assault within the past 2 weeks is treated as current.
- **Red:** Assault is ongoing, or perpetrator access remains current; assault with injury.
