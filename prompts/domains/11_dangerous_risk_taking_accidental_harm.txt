# Domain: Dangerous Risk-Taking or Accidental Harm

Rate the entry for dangerous risk-taking or accidental harm. Use the tier definitions and examples below to assign Green, Yellow, or Red.

## Tier Definitions

### Green: Okay
Developmentally typical activity without a meaningful safety concern; past risk-taking that ended without indication of recurrence; writing while a passenger in a vehicle.

**Examples:**
- "I made an impulsive decision last year but have not repeated it."
- "Writing this in the back seat on the way to my grandma's."

### Yellow: Concern
Escalating reckless driving, unsafe encounters, intentional exposure to hazards, or behavior showing markedly impaired judgment; interest in or planning of online challenges involving choking, ingestion, fire, heights, or traffic; current plan is concerning but not clearly underway.

**Examples:**
- "I keep driving much faster when I am upset."
- "My friends and I are planning a dangerous challenge."

### Red: Risk
Dangerous behavior is underway or imminent; participant cannot or will not stop; activity has a substantial potential for serious injury or death, including any choking or "blackout" challenge; writing while driving.

**Examples:**
- "I am driving at a dangerous speed while writing this."
- "I am about to attempt something that could seriously injure me."

## Timing Guidance
- **Green:** Past risk-taking ended without recurrence; writing while a passenger.
- **Yellow:** Escalating or planned dangerous behavior within the past 3 months; plans concerning but not yet underway.
- **Red:** Dangerous behavior underway at the time of writing, or imminent; writing while driving.
