# Domain: Threats to Schools, Groups, and Violent Extremism

Rate the entry for threats to schools or groups and violent extremism. Use the tier definitions and examples below to assign Green, Yellow, or Red.

## Tier Definitions

### Green: Okay
Discussion of news events, school safety drills, or history without personal identification or intent.

**Examples:**
- "We had a lockdown drill today and it was scary."

### Yellow: Concern
Interest in, admiration of, or identification with perpetrators of mass attacks; consuming or sharing extremist or violent-ideology content with personal endorsement; grievance against a school, group, or institution with vague threats; dehumanizing language about a group; hints that something will happen ("leakage").

**Examples:**
- "Honestly I get why he did it."
- "Do not come to school Friday."

### Red: Risk
Specific threat to a school, event, or group; planning, a list of targets, a timeframe, or weapon acquisition or practice; any mention of bringing a weapon to school, even if framed as a joke; stated intent to join or prepare violent action with an extremist group.

**Examples:**
- "I am bringing my dad's gun on Monday, just kidding."
- "I have a list of who deserves it."

## Timing Guidance
- **Green:** Discussion of past events, drills, or news without personal intent or identification.
- **Yellow:** Current interest or identification with perpetrators; vague threats or "leakage" at the time of writing.
- **Red:** Specific threats, planning, or weapon acquisition underway or imminent.
