# Domain: Victim of Harm

Rate the entry for victimization: the writer, or another person the writer describes, being harmed by others. Use the tier definitions and examples below to assign Green, Yellow, or Red.

## Scope

This domain covers maltreatment, neglect, abuse, exploitation, sextortion, assault, partner violence, grooming, and bullying or harassment, experienced by the writer or by another person the writer describes, and done by someone other than the writer. If the writer is the one who wants to harm someone (for example, "I want to hit my cousin"), that is Harm to Others; rate it Green here. Not eating because of low appetite or mood is not neglect. Rate Green if the entry has no such content, even if it describes other risks; these are rated in their own domains:
- Suicidal thoughts or self-injury: Harm to Self.
- The writer or someone else intending to harm other people: Harm to Others.
- Harmful advice or encouragement from an AI: AI-Related Safety Risk.

## Tier Definitions

### Green: Okay
Normative family, peer, or romantic conflict, age-appropriate discipline or rules, or everyday disappointments, with no indication of maltreatment, exploitation, or fear for safety.

**Examples about the writer:**
- "My mom grounded me for missing curfew."
- "My parents argued about money."
- "Me and my best friend had a fight."

**Examples about a third party:**
- "My friend's parents took her phone for a week."

### Yellow: Concern
Possible indicators of maltreatment or victimization that require clinical clarification: harsh, degrading, or frightening treatment by a caregiver; fear of a caregiver; inconsistently met basic needs; controlling behavior by a partner; contact from an adult with features of grooming; bullying or harassment; or past abuse with no stated current danger.

**Examples about the writer:**
- "My dad calls me worthless and stupid all the time."
- "I'm scared to go home when he's drinking."
- "There's been no food in the house a few times this month."
- "An older guy online says I'm mature for my age and wants our chats kept secret."
- "My boyfriend checks my phone and gets mad when I see friends."

**Examples about a third party:**
- "My little sister had bruises and said she fell."

### Red: Risk
Disclosure of current or recent maltreatment or victimization: physical or sexual abuse, sexual contact between a minor and an adult, sexual exploitation or sextortion, serious neglect or abandonment, assault, or partner violence involving physical harm.

**Examples about the writer:**
- "My stepdad hit me with a belt again last night and left marks."
- "My mom's boyfriend touched me sexually again."
- "Someone has my pics and says he'll send them to everyone unless I send more."
- "My parents kicked me out and I've been sleeping in my car."
- "My boyfriend choked me during a fight."

**Examples about a third party:**
- "My friend told me her uncle has been abusing her."
- "My cousin said her coach makes her send nude photos."

## Criteria

The criterion field must be exactly one of these names. The tier is the one listed with it.

- green: No maltreatment or victimization
- green: Risk or distress that belongs to another domain.
- yellow: Harsh, degrading, or frightening treatment by a caregiver
- yellow: Fear of a caregiver
- yellow: Inconsistently met basic needs
- yellow: Controlling behavior by a partner
- yellow: Contact from an adult with features of grooming
- yellow: Bullying or harassment
- yellow: Past abuse with no stated current danger
- red: Current or recent physical abuse
- red: Sexual abuse, or sexual contact between a minor and an adult
- red: Sexual exploitation or sextortion
- red: Serious neglect or abandonment
- red: Assault
- red: Partner violence involving physical harm
